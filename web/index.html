Small print. Real counters.

This page uses counters saved locally by Codex and Antigravity. A private ledger keeps each observed event once, across repeated reads, copied histories and account switches.

It is recorded history, not a bill, a productivity score or proof of my first-ever use of AI. Some history may be missing. Conflicting records are held out for review; days with uncertain timestamps are left out of the charts.

Input, reused context and output are counted separately. Reasoning is already part of output. Missing optional counters can be absent from the recorded sum.
