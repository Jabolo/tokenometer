Small print. Real counters.

Usage comes from counters saved on my Mac by Codex and Antigravity. Each event is counted once across repeated reads, copied histories and account switches. Reasoning is already included in output.

Some history and optional counters may be missing, including Antigravity fields. Missing counters are not estimated; conflicting records are held out. These totals are not bills or productivity scores.

Agent figures cover saved local history across projects, regardless of the chart's date range. Codex counts saved subagent sessions, not completed tasks. Antigravity counts child conversations linked to parents as of the displayed read; those links do not verify subagent launches. Repeated reads and account copies count once. Agent counts refresh daily.

Recent use counts days with a dated record in the 30 complete days before the selected tools' latest saved read. Missing records do not prove no use. Freshness uses successful source reads, not the export time; the combined view uses the oldest selected read.
